## 2026-07-29 - [GitHub Direct Messaging Reporting Channel Failure]

**Vulnerability:** Contact instructions in SECURITY.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md instructed users to direct message the maintainer on GitHub.
**Learning:** GitHub does not support direct private messaging, meaning users could not privately report security vulnerabilities or code of conduct violations if the primary reporting channel failed or they preferred direct contact. This could lead to public disclosure of vulnerabilities or failure to report issues.
**Prevention:** Always verify that proposed communication channels actually exist and support the required level of privacy. When providing contact instructions, direct users to an email address (e.g., the one listed on the GitHub profile) or another supported secure private messaging platform.
