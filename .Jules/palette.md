## 2024-12-10 - Improve alternative text for image badges
**Learning:** Image badges (e.g., Shields.io) in documentation often lack sufficient context in their alt text for screen reader users, especially when they act as functional links (like pointing to latest releases) or categorize specific technologies.
**Action:** Expand the alternative text of image badges to include their category (e.g., "programming language badge") and include the destination context when wrapped in anchor tags (e.g., "[App Name] latest release") to ensure complete accessibility for screen readers.
